04 Module
Core Skill
Prompt Engineering. The Skill That Makes Everything Else Work
For Getting Finished, Usable Business Output Every Time
Objective

Participants move from random prompting to a structured method that gets finished, usable business output on the first or second try, every time.

Outcome
What Participants Can Do

Every participant leaves with the RCTF framework memorised and at least one business prompt they personally built and tested during the session.

Topics Covered
  • Why most people get bad results from AI and it has nothing to do with the tool. It is entirely the prompt.
  • The RCTF framework: Role, Context, Task, Format. One structure that works for every business task, every platform, every use case.
  • Live demo: the same task prompted badly vs prompted well. The room sees the difference in real time and votes on the output.
  • Hands-on practice: every participant writes a prompt for a real task from their own business or family business. Rishi corrects live.
  • Business prompt templates that work immediately: follow-up emails, vendor messages, pitch scripts, social content, business plans, and investor summaries.
